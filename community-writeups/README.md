# Community / archive writeups (not WhyN@m31t's work)

These documents come from the archive `2026-chronos-security.zip` provided to us
after CSCTF 2026. They are **not** the work of Team WhyN@m31t and are included for
reference only. Authorship, where stated in the documents, is unclear (one file lists
`author: unknown`); the challenges are from Chronos Security (CSCTF 2026).

What is included: the writeups and solver scripts from that archive, with flags
redacted to `CSCTF{REDACTED}` and instance hosts/URLs removed.

What is deliberately **not** included: the challenge artifacts themselves (binaries,
firmware, packet captures, audio, model files, nested archives, sample data) and any
`__pycache__` files — these remain the challenge authors' materials and are not
redistributed here.

Content may still contain documentation-level details (e.g. `CSCTF{...}` flag-format
placeholders and f-string templates in solver code).
